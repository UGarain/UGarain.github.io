Prof. Garain received his Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Jadavpur University in 1994 (WBJEE Rank 22), followed by a Master's degree in the same discipline in 1997. He earned his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) in 2005. Before joining the Indian Statistical Institute as a faculty member, he gained industry experience through brief tenures at Larsen & Toubro Ltd. and Cognizant Technology Solutions.


His research focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, and their applications to language technologies and vision-based analytics. He has authored over 150 research papers in peer-reviewed journals and conferences and holds 12 patents. His contributions have been recognized with several prestigious honors, including the Young Engineer Award from the Indian National Academy of Engineering (INAE) in 2006, the Indo–US Science and Technology Forum Research Fellowship in 2011, and the JSPS Fellowship from the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) in 2017. He was elected a Fellow of the Indian National Academy of Engineering (INAE) in 2023 and a Fellow of the West Bengal Academy of Science and Technology (WAST) in 2024.
