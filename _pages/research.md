## Research Areas
Prof. Utpal Garain's research spans both the theoretical and practical aspects of Machine Learning, particularly Deep Learning, with a focus on Language Engineering, Computer Vision, Computational Genomics, and interdisciplinary AI. His current research interests include:

* Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)
* Adversarial Attacks on Deep Learning Systems
* Deep Learning for Medical Research
* Large Language Models (LLMs)
* Causal Machine Learning
* Computational Genomics
* Medical Image Analysis
* Calibration of Machine Learning Models

Previously, Prof. Garain's research focused on:
* Document Image Analysis (DIA)
* Natural Language Processing (NLP), with a special focus on lemmatization and machine translation
* Information Retrieval
* Pattern Recognition, particularly Handwritten Text Recognition (HTR) and Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
