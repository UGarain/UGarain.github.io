Prof. Garain has been recognized with several prestigious awards and honors for his outstanding contributions to research and academic excellence.


## Professional Awards and Honors

* **2024** – Elected **Fellow** of the **West Bengal Academy of Science and Technology (WAST)**.
* **2023** – Elected **Fellow** of the **Indian National Academy of Engineering (INAE)**.
* **2016–2017** – Awarded the **JSPS Invitational Fellowship** to conduct research at **Osaka University, Japan**.
* **2011** – Received the **Indo–US Research Fellowship** in Engineering Science to conduct research at the **University of Maryland, College Park, USA**.
* **2009** – Awarded **Google Guest** by **Google Inc.** to present research at **ICDAR 2009**.
* **2008** – Awarded **Google Guest** by **Google Inc.** to present research at **ACM SIGIR 2008**.
* **2007** – Invited to deliver a **conference tutorial** at the **9th International Conference on Document Analysis and Recognition (ICDAR 2007)**, Brazil.
* **2007 & 2009** – Nominated for the **IAPR/ICDAR Young Investigator Award**.
* **2006** – Received the prestigious **Young Engineer Award** from the **Indian National Academy of Engineering (INAE)** for outstanding contributions to **Pattern Recognition**, **Digital Image Analysis**, and **Language Engineering**.
* **2004** – Awarded the **EGIDE (CNRS) Postdoctoral Fellowship** to conduct research at **Laboratory PSI, University of Rouen, France**. He also received a **Research Associate** offer from the **University of Central Florida (UCF), USA**, which could not be accepted after committing to the fellowship in France.
