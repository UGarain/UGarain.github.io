activities

Over the course of his academic career, Prof. Garain has delivered numerous invited talks at national and international venues, organized and chaired several conferences and workshops, and served as a reviewer and program committee member for many of the world’s leading journals and conferences in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, and pattern recognition.

Invited Talks

He has delivered 25+ invited talks at leading universities, research laboratories, and academic institutions across France, Germany, Spain, the United States, Singapore, South Korea, and India. His lectures have covered a broad spectrum of topics, including artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, document image analysis, optical character recognition (OCR), pattern recognition, compressed-domain document processing, language technologies, artificial immune systems, digital libraries, and computational imaging.

Invitations have come from internationally renowned institutions such as the University of Maryland, University of Rouen, Technical University of Kaiserslautern, Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, Nanyang Technological University, and numerous premier universities and research organizations across India.

Reviewer and Program Committee Roles

He has served as a reviewer for several leading international journals, including IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence (TPAMI), IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics (SMC), IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation (TEC), IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems (TNNLS), International Journal on Document Analysis and Recognition (IJDAR), Pattern Recognition (PR), Pattern Recognition Letters (PRL), and Image and Vision Computing (IVC).

He has also served as a reviewer and meta-reviewer for premier international conferences, including ICPR, ICDAR, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, COLING, MICCAI, ISBI, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, and AAAI. In addition, he has been a Program Committee (PC) member for several flagship conferences, including AAAI, ICDAR, and ICPR.

Conference and Workshop Organization

He has contributed extensively to the organization of international conferences and workshops in leadership roles. These include serving as Publication Chair for ICFHR 2010, contributing to the organization of ICONIP 2004, and acting as Organizing Convener of the Platinum Jubilee ISI International Conference on Computing: Theory and Applications (2007). He has also been actively involved in organizing the Indo-French Workshop on Statistics and Artificial Intelligence for Data Science (SAIDS), as well as several international workshops on Document Analysis, Technology Development in Indian Languages, and Image Processing in Forensic Sciences at the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata.
