Motivated students interested in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, and related areas are welcome to pursue research. Prospective Ph.D. students should apply through the official ISI admission process, while Master students may contact directly regarding dissertation opportunities. Project positions are offered subject to funding availability and are filled according to ISI Kolkata recruitment policies. Vacancy announcements will be posted on the [ISI website](https://www.isical.ac.in/public/jobs), this website, and [LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/in/utpal-garain-7b365943).
